# Step 3 — AMSS paid-social stress proxy

## Decision

V10 uses AMSS's public and unchanged `DefaultTraditionalMediaModule` to create
an additional channel named `paid_social_proxy`. This is valid use of AMSS's
documented multi-channel interface. The channel is generated by AMSS, not by
FluxMMM.

The name `proxy` is retained in raw simulator data and provenance. The adapter
maps it to `meta_acquisition_spend` only because the frozen V9 selector expects
that role. We do not describe it as a native AMSS paid-social module, a platform
auction, or a literal replica of any advertising platform.

## Why this is not outcome engineering

The mechanism and reference parameters are declared before generating the
three-channel smoke data, before constructing the external cohort, and before
calculating any Flux economic loss. The proxy cannot be tuned after inspecting
candidate rankings, ROAS truth, or external-validation outcomes. No generated
business may be removed because it is difficult for Flux.

The simulator applies its own exposure delivery, reach, frequency, Hill
response, state migration, sales, and stochastic processes. Flux receives only
the resulting weekly series.

## Stress mechanics

The proxy intentionally combines:

- activity-targeted and favorability-targeted audience membership;
- continuous but uneven pacing;
- persistent flighting;
- positive planning correlation with latent category demand;
- AMSS frequency saturation through a Hill curve;
- activity and brand-favorability state transitions; and
- competition for the same consumer states affected by search and television.

This is a demanding transport case because realized proxy spend is endogenous
to latent market conditions, while Flux never receives the exact market-rate
series.

## Frozen mapping

| Raw AMSS channel | AMSS engine | Frozen V9 role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `paid_social_proxy` | `DefaultTraditionalMediaModule` | `meta_acquisition_spend` |
| `search` | `DefaultSearchMediaModule` | `google_search_nonbrand_spend` |
| `tv` | `DefaultTraditionalMediaModule` | `ctv_spend` |

Search and TV are also archetype mappings: AMSS does not natively separate
brand from nonbrand search, and traditional television is not literally CTV.
The external test concerns transport across independently implemented media
mechanisms, not platform-label equivalence.

## Truth firewall

Candidate fitting and frozen V9 selection cannot access latent market demand,
consumer-state tables, transition matrices, proxy construction parameters,
ROAS calculations, counterfactual outcomes, or AMSS optimization results.
Truth is opened only after all 48 candidate decisions for a business are fixed.

## Current boundary

The reference smoke test validates the three-channel data path only. It creates
no admissible research labels and does not yet freeze the cohort distributions,
counterfactual decision grid, truth precision, or external sample size. Those
items must be frozen before cohort generation.
