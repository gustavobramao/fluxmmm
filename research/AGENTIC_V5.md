# Agentic V5 search contract

Agentic V5 keeps the existing Flux Decision Score and its validation,
evidence, and ROI plausibility gates unchanged. It expands the bounded search
across every parameter already supported by the modeling engine.

The default 192-candidate contract comprises 24 global seeds, 48
channel-response covering-array candidates, 12 paired Advanced challenges, up
to 84 feasibility-aware proposals across three starts, and 24 global or
channel-level champion challenges.

V5 searches estimator family, global and channel-specific geometric or
Weibull carryover, Hill shape and half-saturation, kernel normalization, ridge,
Fourier cycle, time-varying coefficients, kernel smoothness, planning
intensity, evidence calibration mode, prior family, likelihood family, and
Student-t degrees of freedom.

This finds a best-known bounded optimum, not a mathematical proof of the global
optimum. Flux reports coverage, restart agreement, plateau, local-challenge,
and active-boundary diagnostics. Promotion retains the exact winning model
configuration, including every channel-specific response.
